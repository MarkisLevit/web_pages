What Kindred is — and isn't

Kindred is peer emotional support. Our listeners are trained and vetted, but they are not therapists, doctors, or counsellors, and Kindred is not a substitute for medical or mental-health treatment. We're also not a crisis service. If you're in immediate danger or thinking about harming yourself, please contact your local emergency number or a crisis helpline in your country right away — a real person there can help faster than we can.
