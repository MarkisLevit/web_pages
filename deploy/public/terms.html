2. What Kindred is — and what it is not

Kindred is peer emotional support. You book time with a listener whose job is to pay attention to you. That is the whole service.

Our listeners are not therapists, doctors, or counsellors. They do not diagnose, treat, prescribe, or give clinical advice, and Kindred is not a substitute for medical or mental-health treatment.

Kindred is not a crisis service. We cannot help in an emergency. If you are in immediate danger, or thinking about harming yourself, contact your local emergency number or a crisis helpline in your country — someone there can help faster than we can.

If a listener thinks what you need is clinical care, they will say so, and we will help you look for it. That is not us withdrawing the service; it is the service working properly.
