  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) — keeping invoice and tax records.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) — keeping the service secure and stopping abuse.
  • Explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a)) — for the topics you choose. See below.

4. Topics, and why we treat them carefully

When you tell us you want to talk about grief, or loneliness, that says something about your mental and emotional health. Attached to your name and email, it may count as special-category data under Article 9 of the GDPR — a stricter category than ordinary personal data, which we may only handle with your explicit consent.

So: choosing topics is optional, it is only used to match you with a listener, it is never put in a web address, never sent to advertising or analytics services, and never shared with Stripe. You can withdraw consent at any time by writing to us, and we will delete those entries without closing your account.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your data, or a portable export of it;
  • correct anything wrong;
  • delete it (“the right to be forgotten”);
  • restrict or object to how we use it;
  • withdraw a consent you gave, without affecting what we did before you withdrew it.

Write to [PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL] and we will answer within one month. There is no charge. If you think we have got it wrong you can complain to your local data protection authority — in Poland, the [Urząd Ochrony Danych Osobowych (UODO), or the authority where you live].

9. Security

  • The whole site is served over HTTPS.
  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt, never stored in readable form.
  • Card data never touches our server.
  • The administrative view is behind a long random token.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach ever puts you at risk, we will tell you and the regulator, as Articles 33 and 34 require.
