Everything we do is free — because someone pays for it

We don't collect money at the door and we don't charge for day camps. That only works because somebody covers it, month after month.

Day camps for children

Materials, food, prizes and equipment. One day of programme for a whole group costs a few hundred złoty.

The room and equipment

Rent on the space at Sikorskiego, heating, sound system and chairs — a fixed cost every month.

Helping families

Practical help for families in difficulty — groceries, school supplies, a paid-for trip for a child.

Transparency

Before you give

Is my gift tax-deductible in Poland?

Not yet. Donations are currently handled by Solid Rock Mission — registered in the USA with 501(c)(3) status, which makes gifts deductible for US taxpayers.

Polish law allows a deduction only for gifts to organisations carrying out public benefit activity in Poland or the EU/EEA, so a gift to a US organisation is not deductible on a Polish tax return. That changes once the Polish foundation is registered — we'll say so here.

Where exactly does the money go?

Rent and upkeep on the room, materials and food for the children's programmes, music and sound equipment, and practical help for families in difficulty.

Can I choose what my gift funds?

Yes. The "Young Disciples — Poland" fund goes straight to the work in Rzeszów. If you want to fund something specific — one day-camp session, say — write to us and we'll tell you what it costs.

What currency are payments settled in?

The current channels settle in US dollars, so your bank will convert at its own rate and may add a conversion fee. Payments in złoty arrive with the foundation's registration.
