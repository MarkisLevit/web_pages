Transparency

Before you give

Is my gift tax-deductible?

Solid Rock Mission is registered in the United States with 501(c)(3) status. That status makes gifts deductible for US taxpayers.

Polish law only allows a deduction for gifts to organisations carrying out public benefit activity in Poland or another EU/EEA country, so a gift to a US organisation is not deductible on a Polish tax return. If the deduction matters to you, write to us — we can suggest how to give through a partner church or foundation in Poland.

Where exactly does the money go?

To camps and clubs (food, accommodation, transport, materials), to material aid for families — food, medicine, clothing, school supplies — and to supporting full-time missionaries. Missionaries are not paid from a central budget: each is supported by the gifts of their own partners.

Can I choose who my gift helps?

Yes. Every project and every missionary has a separate support link, and the money goes exactly there. If you want to sponsor a specific child, choose the discipleship sponsorship option and we will connect you with a child and send you updates on their progress.

What currency are payments settled in?

GiveLively, DonorSupport and PayPal settle in US dollars. Your bank will convert at its own rate and may add a currency conversion fee. Payments in Polish złoty (transfer, BLIK) are being set up — let us know if that matters to you.

Will I get a receipt?

Yes — the payment platforms send a confirmation to the email address you give at checkout. If you need any additional documentation, write to solidrockmission@gmail.com.
