The discipleship path — seven steps

We do not run one-off events. We build a year-round path a child walks for years — from their first camp to the moment they become a leader and a missionary themselves.

Year-round programs

What a year in a disciple's life looks like

Each step is a program in its own right, but only together do they make the whole. A child who came to their first camp at seven is leading their own group ten years later.

  1. Kingdom Kids Summer Camps

    Week-long camps for children aged 5–15. For most it is their first encounter with the Gospel: games, sport, creative activities, evening meetings and friendships that last.

    Ages 5–15 · summer · 5–7 days

  2. Kingdom Kids Clubs

    Weekly clubs running all year. Practical skills training, community building and consistent spiritual care — children do not disappear from our sight once camp ends.

    Year-round · weekly

  3. Revival Camps

    Four- to six-day camps where young people build a personal foundation of faith. Participants most often point to this as their turning point.

    4–6 days

  4. Fall Discipleship Training

    Spiritual disciplines: prayer, reading the Word on their own, fasting and daily walking with God. We teach habits, not emotions.

    Autumn · quarterly

  5. Winter Discipleship Training

    Forgiveness and inner healing. For children marked by war, separation from a father or the loss of a home, this is the hardest and most important stage of the whole path.

    Winter · quarterly

  6. Spring Discipleship Training

    Identity in Christ and preparation for evangelism — the first time a disciple tells someone else about their own faith.

    Spring · quarterly

  7. Missionary Training

    The final step: leadership development. Teenagers who have walked the whole path learn to lead groups, plan events and take responsibility for the next generation of children.

    For leaders · year-round

Questions

What people ask us most

Who can take part in the programs?

Kingdom Kids camps are for children aged 5–15. Discipleship schools and youth clubs are mainly for teenagers from age 12. We welcome children from Ukrainian and Polish families alike — regardless of whether the family has any connection to a church.

Does it cost anything?

Not for the family. All our programs are funded by the gifts of partners and sponsors. That is what makes it possible for a child whose family could never afford it to go to camp.

What language do you run the sessions in?

In Rzeszów we work mainly in Ukrainian and Russian, because most participants come from refugee families. More and more sessions also run in Polish. Write to us and we will tell you how it works in a specific group.

How do I register my child?

The simplest way is our contact form or a message on Instagram. We usually reply within a few days with dates for the next camps and club meetings.

I want to help as a volunteer — where do I start?

Fill in the volunteer application form. We take people for a single camp as well as those who want to commit longer. More than 350 volunteers have already served with us.

One child. One year. Eighteen dollars a month.

That is enough for a child to walk the whole path — camps, training, clothing, food and a Christmas gift.
