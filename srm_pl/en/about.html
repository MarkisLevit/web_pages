About us

We started with a handful of people and one rented room

Solid Rock Poland is a community in Rzeszów and a foundation currently being registered. We are the Polish part of the international Solid Rock mission.

Our story

The first gathering took place in Rzeszów in February 2025. There were only a few of us. We met on Wednesday evenings at first; Sundays came later.

Over the following months more people joined — neighbours, friends of friends, families with children. Today we run weekly services, a home group, a club for teenagers, women's evenings and free day camps for children.

We are not a large organisation and we don't pretend to be. But what we do, we do consistently — and free of charge for anyone who turns up.

What we believe

Three things that matter most to us

Nobody should be alone

A community isn't a place or an event — it's people who actually know each other, pray for each other, and show up when things are hard.

Help without conditions

Our children's programmes are free and open to everyone — regardless of whether the family has any connection to a church.

You can ask anything

You don't need to know anything or believe anything to come. Questions are welcome, including the uncomfortable ones.
