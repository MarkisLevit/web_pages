The team in Poland

Rzeszów and the surrounding region

After 24 February 2022, Rzeszów became our base. From here we run Sunday clubs, camps and training for children and teenagers from refugee families.

Mission leadership

The ones who started this in 2012

Founders · Director · Evangelist

Slavik & Julia Golovatyuk

They founded Solid Rock Mission in 2012. The first camp gathered 90 children — today more than 20,000 young people have come through the mission's programs in Ukraine, Poland and Germany.

“By partnering with us, our ministry becomes your ministry and we will serve as an extension of your hands and feet in spreading the Gospel.”
