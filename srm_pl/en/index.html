Who we are

A small community doing concrete things

We started in Rzeszów at the beginning of 2025 — with a handful of people, one rented room, and the conviction that nobody here should be on their own.

Today we gather every Sunday, run a home group on Wednesdays, and put on free day camps for children and a club for teenagers. We are the Polish part of the international Solid Rock mission, which has been running since 2012.
