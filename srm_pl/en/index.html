Our mission

There is no greater joy than doing the will of the Father

Jesus discipled, and He called us to make disciples. We reach young people, walk with them in discipleship, and equip them in their gifts and calling for the Kingdom of God.

When the war of 24 February 2022 pushed hundreds of thousands of Ukrainian families into Poland, our ministry moved with them. Rzeszów is now our base — the place we run Sunday clubs, camps and training for teenagers whose childhood the war took away.

Our goal is not only to provide joy, gifts and a good time at camp. Our goal is to see every one of these children in heaven.

The discipleship path

Seven steps — from first camp to ministry

We do not run one-off events. We build a year-round path a child walks for years — until they become a leader and a missionary themselves.

  1. Kingdom Kids Summer Camps

    Week-long camps for children aged 5–15. A first encounter with the Gospel, games, sport and friendships that last.

    Ages 5–15 · summer

  2. Kingdom Kids Clubs

    Weekly clubs all year round — practical skills training, community building and consistent spiritual care.

    Year-round · weekly

  3. Revival Camps

    Four- to six-day camps where young people lay a personal foundation of faith.

    4–6 days

  4. Fall Discipleship Training

    Spiritual disciplines: prayer, the Word, fasting and daily walking with God.

    Autumn

  5. Winter Discipleship Training

    Forgiveness and inner healing — a decisive stage for children marked by war and separation.

    Winter

  6. Spring Discipleship Training

    Identity in Christ and preparation for evangelism — the first time a disciple shares their own faith.

    Spring
