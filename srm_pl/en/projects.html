Young Disciples — Poland

The war has taken away the childhood and joy of many Ukrainian teenagers. Our Sunday clubs bring Gospel teaching, games and fellowship — a place where a teenager can be a teenager again, not a refugee. Based in Rzeszów and expanding to further cities.

Project Second Chance

A project for children and refugees of war. $50 is enough to send one child to camp. Our goal is to reach more than 3,000 children who would otherwise never get the chance.

Humanitarian Aid to Ukraine

We deliver food, medicine and shelter — both to those sheltering in place and to those who fled abroad. Aid reaches families, children, the elderly, and pastors and missionaries. We work in partnership with churches and volunteers in Ukraine, Poland, Germany and Romania.

Sponsor a child's discipleship

Our vision is to disciple a child from a young age until adulthood. Many of our former participants are missionaries and leaders today. One monthly commitment of $18 gives a child a full year of care.

Completed · 2019

Christmas gifts for children

“As we continue to disciple children for the next 5 years our goal is to organize a Christmas program and to get them a Christmas gift.” To date we have handed out more than 10,010 presents.

Completed · 2019

SMBS Ukraine Team 2019

A mission trip to Golubivka. The aim was to show God's love to the people of Ukraine by spending time with them and giving real support to children and those in need.

Completed · 2018

Church and missionary base in Western Ukraine

A fundraiser to buy a building that became the base for a local church and mission team — a place for training, camps and everyday ministry.

Completed · 2017

Sprinter van for kids camps

Having our own transport meant we stopped cancelling trips because no coach was available to hire. The van still carries children to camp today.

Send Maryana to the IHOP-KC internship

Maryana Hrynyk served as a missionary for four years in Znamenivka, leading worship. The fundraiser financed her internship at the International House of Prayer in Kansas City.

Powerful Foundation

Behind every project are specific people

Solid Rock Mission was founded in 2012 by Slavik and Julia Golovatyuk. From a first camp for 90 children to more than 20,000 participants — the mission grew alongside the people who came through it.

“By partnering with us, our ministry becomes your ministry and we will serve as an extension of your hands and feet in spreading the Gospel.”
